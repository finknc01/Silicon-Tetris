# Level 00 — Build the Workload Deck

## Briefing
Every team asks for “GPU capacity.” Their actual needs are different.

## Objective
Turn workload descriptions into resource requirements.

## Tasks
Create 6–10 fictional workload cards spanning training, fine-tuning, batch inference, and latency-sensitive inference. For each record model size assumption, GPU/VRAM need, duration, concurrency, CPU/RAM, storage read/write, network intensity, priority, and deadline/SLA.

## Challenge
Identify two workloads that look similar by GPU count but differ radically in another bottleneck.

## Evidence
- workload cards in YAML/CSV/Markdown
- assumptions glossary
- first resource-demand summary

## Victory condition
You stop treating “number of GPUs” as the complete capacity requirement.
