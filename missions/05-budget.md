# Level 05 — Finance Takes a Turn

## Briefing
The technically ideal design is over budget.

## Objective
Connect cost to useful capacity and quantify tradeoffs.

## Tasks
Assign fictional or clearly sourced costs to candidate server/network/storage options. Calculate cost per usable GPU, cost per workload served, and headroom. Include supporting infrastructure rather than pricing GPUs alone.

## Constraint change
Cut capital budget by 20% while preserving one high-priority SLA. Decide what to defer or redesign.

## Evidence
- BOM/cost model
- options matrix
- sacrificed capability and rationale

## Victory condition
You can explain the difference between cheapest hardware and lowest-cost architecture that meets requirements.
